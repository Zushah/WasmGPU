CT Volume Workflow
A chest CT is cropped with an ndarray view, cast to f32, resampled to isotropic voxels, and normalized on the GPU or the CPU. Planes slice the result, and a compute kernel fills the splat volume. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, or click to probe.
Data: LIDC-IDRI (The Cancer Imaging Archive, CC BY 3.0) via Kitware vtk-js-datasets
